Meet Kavin.

Born and raised under Calgary's wide-open sky, Kavin built ListWithKavin.ca on a conviction that real estate, done right, is one of the most personal services there is. Behind every offer is a family, a milestone, a quiet bet on the future.

Kavin's clients return — and refer — because the work is patient, the strategy is sharp, and the standard is uncompromising. From a $385,000 first home in Bridgeland to a $2.7M heritage estate in Elbow Park, the level of care is identical.

In an industry of transactional thinking, Kavin builds careers, not closings.
