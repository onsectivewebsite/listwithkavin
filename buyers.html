First-Time Buyer?

The Five Truths Every First-Time Calgary Buyer Should Know

01

Pre-Approval First

Never house-hunt without a pre-approval. It's leverage, clarity, and credibility — all in one.

02

5% Down is Real

Calgary qualifies for CMHC 5% down on homes under $500K. There are paths — Kavin will walk yours.

03

Closing Costs Sting

Budget 1.5–4% extra for legal, inspection, GST/PTT, and adjustments. We'll itemize it for you.

04

Inspect, Always

Calgary winters punish hidden defects. A $600 inspection has saved Kavin's clients $60K+ many times.
