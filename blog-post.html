Calgary Just Had Its Strongest Q1 in a Decade — Here's What It Means for You.

By Kavin · 6 min read · Updated May 14, 2026

Calgary's spring market opened with a force not seen since 2014 — and it's reshaping the calculus for every buyer, seller, and investor in the city.

The Calgary Real Estate Board's latest data confirms what Kavin's clients have felt for months: this is the tightest seller's market we've had in a decade. Detached benchmark prices climbed 6.2% year-over-year, while active listings dropped to a thin 1.4 months of supply — a level that historically forces multiple-offer conditions.

The numbers, decoded

Behind the headline price growth, three quieter shifts deserve attention:

  1. Luxury (>$1M) transactions doubled. West Springs, Aspen Woods, and Elbow Park led the way. Out-of-province relocation buyers — many from Toronto and Vancouver — are quietly driving this.
  2. Inner-city condos are finally moving. After three slow years, Beltline and Mission inventory is being absorbed at twice the rate of Q1 2024.
  3. Investor cap rates are tightening — still strong, but no longer the lazy 7%+ yields of 2022. Selection now matters more than ever.

What this means if you're buying

Pre-approvals matter more than ever — and they need to be from a lender Kavin trusts, with a real rate hold. Multiple-offer scenarios will be common through July, so your offer strategy (price, conditions, deposit, escalation) must be set before you walk into showings. We're getting our buyers into homes Saturday morning with offers in by Sunday night.

What this means if you're selling

This is the window. Pricing right, presenting right, and launching right will likely net you 4–8% more than the same listing six months from now. Don't confuse "hot market" with "lazy market" — the homes that overprice still sit, even now.

Kavin's three neighborhoods to watch this summer

Bridgeland — Inner-city walkability with infill supply finally meeting demand. Cap rates and capital appreciation both look strong.

Mahogany — The lake premium is widening, not narrowing. Family buyers paying for lifestyle in ways we haven't seen.

Tuscany — Under-the-radar value for first-time buyers and families. Schools and commute keep the demand sticky.
