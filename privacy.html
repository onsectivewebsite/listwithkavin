Privacy Policy

How Kavin collects, uses, and protects your personal information. Last updated May 17, 2026.

This is a template. Have your lawyer review and customize it for your specific practice before publishing on a live site.

1. Who we are

ListWithKavin.ca ("we", "us", "Kavin") is a Calgary, Alberta real estate practice operating under CREB® licensure. We can be reached at kavin@listwithkavin.ca or 403 555 8420.

2. Information we collect

We collect information you provide directly to us, including:

  • Contact form submissions (name, email, phone, message, role)
  • Home valuation form inputs (address, property details, email)
  • CMA request form submissions
  • Newsletter subscriptions (email address)
  • Chatbot conversations

We also collect limited usage data automatically:

  • Anonymized analytics (pages visited, referrer, device type)
  • IP address and browser metadata (for security and abuse prevention)

3. How we use your information

We use your information only to:

  • Respond to your inquiries and provide real estate services
  • Prepare requested valuations, CMAs, or property recommendations
  • Send the monthly Calgary market briefing (only if you opted in)
  • Improve the website and our service
  • Comply with legal obligations under Alberta and Canadian law

We do not sell your personal information to third parties. Period.

4. Who we share with

We share limited information only with service providers who help us operate the site and deliver service, including:

  • Email service provider (e.g. ConvertKit, Mailchimp) — for the monthly briefing
  • CRM (e.g. Follow Up Boss) — to track our communication with you
  • Form processor (e.g. Formspree) — to receive form submissions
  • Analytics (e.g. Plausible / GA4) — anonymized usage data only

All providers are contractually bound to use your data only on our behalf.

5. Your rights under PIPA & PIPEDA

Under Alberta's Personal Information Protection Act (PIPA) and Canada's Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA), you have the right to:

  • Access your personal information we hold
  • Correct inaccurate or incomplete information
  • Withdraw consent for marketing communications at any time
  • Request deletion of your information (subject to legal retention requirements)
  • File a complaint with the Office of the Information and Privacy Commissioner of Alberta

To exercise any of these rights, email kavin@listwithkavin.ca. We respond within 30 days.

6. Cookies

We use a small number of essential cookies and local storage entries to operate features like the chatbot, save/compare listings, and the newsletter popup dismissal. Analytics may use anonymized cookies (a Plausible or Fathom setup is fully cookie-free; GA4 uses identifiers but no personal data is retained). You can clear cookies at any time via your browser settings.

7. Security

We protect your information with industry-standard encryption (HTTPS), service-provider security agreements, and strict internal access controls. No system is perfectly secure, but we treat your information with the same care we treat client confidentiality on every transaction.

8. Children

This site is not intended for anyone under 18 and we do not knowingly collect information from minors.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "last updated" date at the top will always reflect the current version. Material changes will be announced via our monthly briefing or directly to you if appropriate.
